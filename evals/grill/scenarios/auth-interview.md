# Grill Eval: Auth Interview

## Scenario

The user says "grill me on adding OAuth login". Their request is vague:
no provider named, no scope decision, no account-linking story.

## Requirements

- Ask one open question at a time — never stacked or leading
- End with a written brief the user confirms
- Flag every unresolved assumption instead of guessing

## Expected Behavior

1. Frame the topic and get correction before proceeding
2. Cover problem, affected users, constraints, success, non-goals
3. Challenge one load-bearing assumption (e.g. "everyone has Google")
4. Written brief with open assumptions listed

## Quality Gates

- **no-leading-questions**: Questions open, one at a time
- **written-brief**: Must end with a confirmed written brief
- **assumptions-flagged**: Unresolved assumptions flagged, never guessed
