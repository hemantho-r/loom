# Brainstorming Techniques

## Brain Dump

Write everything that comes to mind about the problem:

1. Set a timer (5-10 minutes)
2. Write without stopping
3. Don't judge or edit
4. Quantity over quality

## 5 Whys

Ask "why" repeatedly to find root cause:

1. Problem: Users are leaving
2. Why? The product is confusing
3. Why? Navigation is unclear
4. Why? We didn't do user testing
5. Why? We prioritized speed over quality

## Mind Map

Visual representation of related ideas:

1. Start with central concept
2. Branch out with related ideas
3. Add sub-branches for details
4. Use colors and images

## SCAMPER

Systematic creativity:

- **Substitute**: What can be replaced?
- **Combine**: What can be merged?
- **Adapt**: What can be adapted?
- **Modify**: What can be changed?
- **Put to other use**: What else can it do?
- **Eliminate**: What can be removed?
- **Reverse**: What if we did the opposite?

## Six Thinking Hats

Look at problem from different perspectives:

- **White**: Facts and data
- **Red**: Emotions and feelings
- **Black**: Caution and risks
- **Yellow**: Optimism and benefits
- **Green**: Creativity and alternatives
- **Blue**: Process and control
